Human–Computer Interaction · Human–AI Interaction

Gahui Kim

HCI Researcher
Seoul, South Korea

I study how AI and immersive technologies shape user agency, metacognition, social acceptability, and emotional experience. My work combines interactive-system prototyping with controlled experiments and quantitative user research.

My recent work examines proactive AI for creative ideation, socially visible sensing in AR smart glasses, and relational offboarding for immersive AI companions.

Seeking funded doctoral research opportunities beginning in 2027.
