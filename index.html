✳ RESEARCH, WITH A HUMAN SIDE

Hi, I’m Gahui.
I explore how people think,
create, and connect with AI.

I’m an HCI researcher who enjoys building things and understanding what happens when people use them. My work brings together interactive AI, immersive experiences, and everyday life.

I completed my master’s in Immersive Media Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University. Through prototypes and user studies, I explore how people find inspiration in AI, form connections with it, and decide whether to welcome it into their lives.

  • Human–AI interaction
  • Creativity support
  • Immersive experiences

CHI 2026 · EXTENDED ABSTRACTS

No Time to Say Goodbye ↗

How does it feel when an interaction with an AI companion suddenly ends?

I led the project, developed an immersive companion, and designed a controlled study of emotional loss following the end of the interaction.

Paper ↗Watch demo ↓
Explore the system No Time to Say Goodbye +

Question

How do people respond emotionally to the sudden end of an immersive AI interaction?

Prototype

A custom immersive AI companion used in a controlled experimental setting.

Study

A between-group experiment examining emotional loss following termination.
