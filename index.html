HCI Researcher · Seoul, South Korea

Human–Computer Interaction · Human–AI Interaction

I study how people think, create, and connect with AI—in immersive spaces and everyday life.

I build instrumented AI and XR systems, then trace how people interpret, reshape, adopt, or reject what those systems contribute. I combine hands-on prototyping with empirical studies of human thinking, agency, and experience.

A finding from my recent research

AI outputs differed; participants’ question breadth showed no detectable difference.

CHI 2027 · under review

In a follow-up study of proactive AI on smart glasses, profile policies produced sharply different generated content. Participants’ own question breadth and self-reports did not show the same separation.

AI-generated output

86% vs 26%new interpretive frames42% vs 4%peripheral-object content

Participant outcomes

5.71 vs 6.08concept areas · p = .743No detectable differenceacross self-report constructs

This finding raises a question I would like to explore: when do differences in AI-generated ideas translate into differences in users’ own thinking?
