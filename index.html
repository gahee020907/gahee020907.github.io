About

Research grounded in systems, studies, and lived experience.

I am a researcher at AXIS Lab, where I work across human–AI interaction and immersive media. My recent research examines socially visible sensing in AR smart glasses, proactive AI for creative ideation, and emotional loss when interactions with immersive AI companions end.

My work combines prototype development with controlled experiments and quantitative user research. I am especially interested in systems that adapt to people while preserving their ability to understand, evaluate, and shape what the system is doing.
